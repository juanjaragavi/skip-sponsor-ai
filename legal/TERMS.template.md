## 2. What SkipSponsor AI does

SkipSponsor AI looks for sponsor reads in the captions of the YouTube video
you are watching and skips them according to your settings. It is free: there
is no account, no subscription and no payment. Each installation has a daily
allowance of new analyses; videos that were already analyzed do not count
against it.

<!-- COUNSEL-REVIEW ss-terms-2: disclaimer for automated (AI) detection; not affiliated with YouTube or Google. -->

Detection is automatic and uses an AI model. It can be wrong: it can skip
something that is not a sponsor read or miss one that is. You can undo a skip,
turn skipping off for a channel, or switch the extension off at any time.
