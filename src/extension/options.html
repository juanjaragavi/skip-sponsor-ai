Privacy

Plain-language summary of what SkipSponsor AI reads and keeps.

Only caption text is analyzed, never audio

The video’s captions are read to find where a sponsor read begins and ends.

No account, no personal data

Works without sign-in. An anonymous install token protects the service from abuse.

Results are cached for 30 days, then deleted

Segment results are shared per video so repeat views are instant.

Videos without captions aren’t analyzed

SkipSponsor AI reads only the video’s own captions. When a video has none, it says so and the video plays normally.
