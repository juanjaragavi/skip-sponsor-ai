SkipSponsor AI — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026 · Version 2026-10-28 · Applies to SkipSponsor AI 1.1.0 and later

SkipSponsor AI is a browser extension that skips sponsor reads inside YouTube videos. To find a sponsor read, it analyzes the caption text of the video you are watching. This policy explains what is processed, why, where, for how long, and how to exercise your rights. The English and Spanish versions of this policy have equal standing.

SkipSponsor AI is provided by Devspeak S.A.S. (DevSpeak Labs, "we", "us"), a simplified stock company (S.A.S.) incorporated in Colombia (Cll. 7D # 43A - 40, Piso 8, Medellín, Antioquia 050022, Colombia). Devspeak S.A.S. is the controller of the data described here: the company that decides why and how it is processed. We apply this policy together with the data protection laws that apply to you.

  • Email for privacy questions and requests: info@devspeak.dev
  • Website: https://labs.devspeak.dev

Summary

  • Only caption text is analyzed: never audio, never video frames.
  • No account, no sign-in, no name, no email. The extension does not read your YouTube or Google account, cookies, comments, or watch-history pages.
  • It only runs on https://www.youtube.com/.
  • It counts installs, uninstalls and days used with a random ID that is not linked to you, so we know how many people use it. You can turn this off.
  • Analysis results are stored per video, not per person, for 30 days, then deleted.
  • Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used for any purpose unrelated to skipping sponsor segments.

What is not collected

Your name, email, Google or YouTube account, cookies, audio, video frames, browsing activity outside www.youtube.com, YouTube search queries, comments, the content of any other website, and your location. We do not build profiles of people.

Where data goes

All traffic uses HTTPS.

  1. Our server. The SkipSponsor AI backend, operated by Devspeak S.A.S. on Amazon Web Services in the United States (region us-east-2, Ohio), receives the data in the table above. Amazon Web Services hosts the server, its database and its backups on our behalf. For a video it has no result for, the server may also ask YouTube for that video's public caption track, sending only the video ID.
  2. Anthropic (Claude API). When a video has no stored result, our server sends its caption text and timestamps only (no install token, no IP address, no settings) to Anthropic's API, which classifies the sponsor segments. Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, except where it must keep them longer to enforce its usage policy or to comply with the law (Anthropic Privacy Center). We have no zero-retention agreement with Anthropic.

No other third party receives data. The extension contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. The anonymous usage counts described above go only to our server. Videos without captions are not analyzed: nothing about their content is sent anywhere, and the extension tells you the video wasn't analyzed.

The backend contains a SponsorBlock lookup that is switched off. It would only be turned on with SponsorBlock's written permission, and this policy would be updated before that happens.

Legal basis

We process the data described here to provide the feature you installed the extension for, and, for skip statistics, anonymous usage counts and abuse protection, on our legitimate interest in keeping the service accurate and secure. You can object to both by turning them off (see "Your choices"). Where the law that applies to you requires your authorization or consent, you give it by installing and using the extension after being informed by its Chrome Web Store listing and this policy, and you can withdraw it at any time by turning the extension off or uninstalling it.

International transfers

Our server and Anthropic's API process data mainly in the United States. This means your data may be processed outside the country where you live, under laws that may protect personal data differently. Each provider is bound by terms that require it to protect the data and to use it only to provide its service to us, and we give each one only what it needs. You can ask us for more information at info@devspeak.dev.

How to exercise them. Email info@devspeak.dev with your request. Complaints. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work, or of the country where we are established. Please contact us first, so we can try to solve it.

Because we do not link stored data to a person, we usually cannot find data "about you" on our server: results are stored per video, and skip events carry no token or IP address. Usage counts are stored only as a one-way hash of a random ID, so they cannot be traced back to your browser. Include the video IDs you are asking about. The data in your browser is under your control (see below).

Your choices

  • Turn skipping off with the switch in the extension popup, or choose Off mode. Both stop skipping right away.
  • Turn skipping off for a single channel ("Don't skip this channel" in the popup, or Settings › Channels).
  • Stop sending usage statistics: Settings › Privacy › Share usage statistics. Skipping keeps working; no skip or undo events and no usage counts are sent, and no page opens when you uninstall.
  • Erase everything this browser holds (settings, channel list, anonymous install token and usage ID): Settings › Privacy › Reset SkipSponsor AI.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes your settings, install token and usage ID from your browser. If usage statistics are on, Chrome opens a short thank-you page that counts the uninstall.
  • To ask us to delete the stored result for a specific video, email info@devspeak.dev with the video ID. Results are deleted automatically after 30 days in any case.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information received through the extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use the data only to provide and improve the skipping of sponsor segments, we do not transfer it except as described above, we never use it for advertising or to determine creditworthiness, and no person reads it except for security reasons, to comply with the law, or in aggregated form.

Security

All traffic between the extension and the backend uses HTTPS. The extension runs under Chrome's Manifest V3 rules: it contains no remotely hosted code and uses only the storage permission and access to https://www.youtube.com/*. Secrets used by the backend (such as the Anthropic API key) are kept as server-side secrets and never ship in the extension. The source code of the extension and the backend is public at https://github.com/juanjaragavi/skip-sponsor-ai.

Children

SkipSponsor AI is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
